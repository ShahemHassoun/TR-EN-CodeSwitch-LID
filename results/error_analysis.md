## Analysis

MIXED F1 rises from 0.300 to 0.444 for these runs. Against the gold labels, the augmented run fixes 4 tokens (culotte'lar (gold label looks wrong; 1 token(s)), demo (other; 2 token(s)), telegram (name vs MIXED; 1 token(s))), degrades 1, and leaves 10 persistently wrong. The clearest corrected patterns are false-positive reductions on `telegram`, `demo`; the gold label for `culotte'lar` is flagged for review, so that apparent fix is not strong evidence. This split provides no evidence that synthetic data resolved suffix generalization on bare English stems: `appletti`, `downloadla`, `folku`, `linki`, `webin` remain wrong in both models. Persistent apostrophe and NE/MIXED confusions, plus the organization-name regression, show the remaining limits. Counts are specific to this seed and test split.
