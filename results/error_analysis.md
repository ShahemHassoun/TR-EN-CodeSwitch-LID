## Analysis

Adding 5,000 synthetic sentences raises `MIXED` F1 from 0.300 to 0.471 on the test split (precision doubles from 0.333 to 0.667, and recall improves from 0.273 to 0.364). Across the 18 MIXED-focused tokens, `berturk_gen5000_seed1` fixes 6 errors, degrades 1, leaves 9 persistently wrong, and keeps 2 correct in both runs. Specifically, synthetic data successfully eliminates false-positive `MIXED` predictions on bare English stems (`demo`) and named entities (`telegram`, `jslerle`), while beginning to resolve un-apostrophed Turkish suffixation on English stems (`downloadla`, `mottom`). However, bare stems without apostrophes remain the dominant failure mode (`linki`, `appletti`, `webin`, `folku`), alongside persistent `NE` vs `MIXED` boundary confusions on rare apostrophed forms (`pseudocide'ı`, `views'teki`, `culotte'lar`) and one likely gold-label inconsistency (`textin` labeled `TR`).
